Admission Process

  1. Complete the online admission application form.
  2. Upload all required academic documents.
  3. Pay the application processing fee.
  4. Attend the admission test or interview (if applicable).
  5. Receive the admission decision and offer letter.
  6. Complete enrollment by paying the tuition fee and registering for classes.
